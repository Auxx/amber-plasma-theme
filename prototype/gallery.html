How far the focused tone stands from the widget at rest and from the surface (O-3). A button at rest beside a focused one.

Focused well

How far the focused tone stands from the widget at rest and from the surface (O-3). The placeholder in dim text must keep 4.5:1.

Empty well

An empty well against its surface (O-1). No darker well can pass 1.33:1 on tone 2, so a stronger cue is lighter or is a well edge.

Focused toggle

How far the focused tone stands from the toggle at rest and from the surface (O-3). A toggle holds no text, so its tint is not capped by the placeholder.

Unchecked toggle

An unchecked toggle against its surface (O-1). A lighter box is the cue a darker one cannot give.

Track

An empty track against its surface, and the fill against its track (O-1). A track at 3:1 drops the fill below 3:1.
