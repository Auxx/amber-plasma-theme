# P3.8 Popups Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** The Union style draws menus, menu items, menu sections, combo box lists, tooltips and dialogs in the prototype's tones and sizes, in every state, with space and no line dividing a menu, and with the popup's shadow where Union can draw one. This is checked by pixels in a Qt Widgets application, on screen and not only through `render()`, and by eye in Dolphin and Kate.

**Architecture:** This plan rewrites every block of `popup.css` except `menubar` and `menubaritem` (P3.6's), the `menuitem` and `menuitem.exclusive` blocks of `check.css` (left there by P3.5), and whatever element Task 1 finds for a combo box's list. They use only the variables of the generated `tokens.css` (D-97), and `--shadow` if Task 1 finds that a shadow is drawn. The check is P3.3's to P3.7's: `browser states` gives the gallery's expectation, the probe draws each state, and `image states --region` compares one part of a popup. Task 1 first answers what P3.2 and P3.3 could not: how to put a popup on screen in the nested session, since `QMenu.popup()` and `QComboBox.showPopup()` from a script show nothing on Wayland (platform.md, "Union"). It also answers the two open questions that platform.md gives to P3.8: whether a shadow is drawn under a popup in Qt Widgets, and why a combo box's list is transparent. Task 2 adds to the prototype the states it does not show yet: a pressed and a checked hovered menu item, an exclusive item, an open submenu, a menu section, a combo box list's states and a tooltip row.

**Tech Stack:** Union CSS (`plasma-union` 6.8.2), Node 24 with `node:test`, the `browser`, `image`, `generate` and `tokens` tools, PySide6 6.11.2, the nested `kwin_wayland` session of P2.3 to P3.7 (`tmp/check-colour-scheme/`), Dolphin, Kate.

**Spec:** `docs/product/roadmap.md` (row P3.8 and the notes under Step 3: a menu seen on screen, the transparent combo box list), `docs/architecture/design-language.md` ("Surfaces": popups; "Widgets": the menu item row and the bullets on menus, tooltips and dialogs; "Shadow"; the token `--popup`), `prototype/widgets.css` (the "Popups" rules: `.popup`, `.menu`, `.menu-item`, `.menu-tick`, `.menu-shortcut`, `.menu-arrow`, `.menu-gap`, `.combo-open`, `.tooltip`), `prototype/window.css` (the popup's shadow), `prototype/gallery.html` (the family "Popups": rows `menu-title` and `menu-item`, and the live menu, combo box list and tooltip; the family "Dialog"), `docs/architecture/platform.md` ("Union": what Union reaches on the bars, a popup cannot be opened from a script, the two open questions for P3.8; "Colour scheme": the derived backgrounds, the question for P3.8; P0.3 and P0.5: `box-shadow` in Qt Widgets), `docs/architecture/decisions.md` (D-49, D-59, D-67, D-74, D-95, D-97, D-98, D-105, D-108, D-112), `docs/superpowers/plans/P3.7-lists-and-tables.md` (the probe's regions, and the outcome).

## Global Constraints

- Branch `feat/p3.8-popups` from `master`. This plan file is committed on it.
- Use the glossary's words in British spelling: popup, menu (new, Task 2), menu item (new, Task 2), menu section (new, Task 2), menu title, combo box, tooltip, dialog, message (new, Task 2), tick, mark, shadow, surface, tone, at rest, hovered, focused, pressed, checked, disabled, inactive, known gap. Never "separator" for the 8 px gap in prose, "check mark", "context menu item" or "message widget", except in Union's own selectors (`menuitem`, `menuseparator`, `.with-title`, `.with-submenu`, `shortcuttext`) and Qt's or KDE's names (`QMenu::addSection`, `KMessageWidget`).
- **Never change the owner's system settings.** `sha256sum ~/.config/kdeglobals ~/.config/kwinrc ~/.config/dolphinrc ~/.config/katerc` gives the same result at the start (Task 1) and at the end (Task 5). Nothing is written under `~/.config`, `~/.local/share` or `/usr`. Opening a menu, a combo box or a settings dialog of Dolphin or Kate is done in the nested session, whose configuration lives in `tmp/check-colour-scheme/config/`.
- **Anti-goals.** No rounded corner, no hairline and no cool grey. Every `border-radius` of the popup blocks is `0px`, the menu item's tick box included; a menu item's radio stays round, as a radio button does (a round widget, P3.5). A menu's divider is an 8 px gap with no fill: `menuseparator` has no background and no border, and is never `1px` high. A menu section has no line under or beside its title. A dialog has no line above its buttons. No popup has an outline. The shadow is dark: `--shadow`, black, never a colour with a hue.
- **The target tones** are the design language's, as the prototype draws them. Each cell gives background / text:

  | Widget | At rest | Hovered | Focused | Pressed | Checked | Disabled |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Menu, combo box list | `popup` | — | — | — | — | `popup` (my call) |
  | Menu item | no fill / `text`; shortcut and submenu arrow `text-dim` | `tone-4` / `text`; shortcut and arrow `text` | as hovered (Qt does not tell the two apart) | as hovered (my call) | no fill / `text`, tick `accent` | no fill / `text-dim`; shortcut `text-dim`; tick `text-dim` (my call) |
  | Exclusive menu item | as a menu item | | | | no fill / `text`, dot `accent` (my call) | as a menu item |
  | Menu item with its submenu open | `tone-4` / `text`, as hovered, while the pointer is in the submenu | | | | | |
  | Menu section's title | no fill / `text-dim`, not bold (my call) | — | — | — | — | — |
  | Combo box list item | as a menu item; the current value carries the tick, as `.combo-open` draws it, if Union draws a tick there (Task 1, (d)) | | | | | |
  | Tooltip | `popup` / `text` | — | — | — | — | — |
  | Dialog's body (Qt Quick `dialog`) | `tone-1` / `text` (my call) | — | — | — | — | — |

  Precedence: pressed beats focused, and focused beats hovered. A disabled widget never changes. Within a block, the state blocks come in the order hovered (`:hovered`, `:highlighted`), focused, pressed, checked, disabled (P3.3: the later block wins). An unchecked checkable menu item shows no box: its tick column is empty.
- **Sizes** (the prototype): a menu item is 26 px high, padded 6 px on the leading side and 12 px on the trailing side. The tick column is 28 px: 6 px, a 16 px tick, 6 px. The shortcut and the submenu arrow sit at least 24 px after the label. A menu is padded 4 px at the top and the foot and 0 at the sides, so a hovered item fills the menu's width. A menu's gap is 8 px. A menu section's title is 26 px high, its text where an item's label starts. A tooltip is padded 4 px at the top and foot and 8 px at the sides, and is at most 240 px wide if Union reaches it. If Union cannot reach a size, record what it reaches and why in `platform.md` and keep the nearest size. This alone is not a reason to stop.
- **The shadow** follows D-67 and D-74. A popup has the shadow of an inactive window (`--shadow`, a fade 36 px long that shows about 9 px above, 19 px beside and 32 px below), and a tooltip a third of it. Union's `box-shadow` is one shadow of four lengths and a colour (platform.md, "Union CSS"), so it cannot draw the eased curve or cut the shadow above the popup's top edge: the rule is the single `box-shadow` whose pixels come nearest, with the values Task 1 measures (my call). If Task 1 finds that no pixel of a popup's `box-shadow` is drawn in a Qt Widgets application, a popup there has no shadow and that is accepted (D-74): the rule is kept for Qt Quick, `platform.md` says so, and it is not a known gap. If KWin or Qt draws a shadow of its own under a popup, Task 1 records whose it is and its colours; a grey in it goes to the owner in Task 4.
- **Decisions of this plan** (my call, recorded as D-114 in Task 5, and drawn in the prototype in Task 2 before the style):
  - **A pressed menu item** is drawn as hovered: the design language gives it no pressed tone, and Breeze's accent fill would flash the accent under every click.
  - **A disabled menu item's tick** is `text-dim`, as a disabled toggle's mark is (D-105).
  - **An exclusive menu item** carries the radio's dot in `accent` with no box, as a checkable item carries the tick.
  - **A menu section's title** is `text-dim` at the item's weight, with no line: the 8 px gap above it and its dim text set it apart.
  - **A disabled menu** keeps `popup`.
  - **A combo box list** is a menu: its items take the menu item's tones and sizes, whichever element Union gives them.
  - **A dialog in Qt Quick**, which Qt Quick draws inside its window, has the body of a dialog window, `tone-1` (D-95), and the popup's shadow. A dialog window in Qt Widgets is drawn by the colour scheme and the decoration and takes no rule here. `overlay.modal` keeps Breeze's black at 30 %, which darkens a warm tone without turning it grey.
  - The inactive window changes no popup.
- **Contrast pairs.** Every pair this family uses must exist and pass: `text` on `popup` and `tone-4`; `text-dim` on `popup` and `tone-4`; `accent` on `popup` and `tone-4` (the tick and the dot, 3:1); `text` and `text-dim` on `tone-1`. `node tools/src/bin/tokens.mjs pairs` passes. A missing pair is added in `prototype/tokens.css` in Task 2. `--shadow` carries no text and has no pair. No new token is expected beyond adding `--shadow` to the Union style's tokens; if another is needed, it goes to the owner first.
- **Stop and ask the owner** before Task 3 if Task 1 finds that a combo box's list stays transparent whatever rule is set, or that Union gives a hovered menu item no state, or that a menu's divider, a section's line or a popup's rounded corner is drawn by Qt or KWin and no rule removes it. An unreadable list or a menu with no visible item changes what Amber is, and a line or rounded corner is an anti-goal (CLAUDE.md, "Stop and ask"). Offer what Task 1 found. Ask too if no way is found to put a popup on screen in the nested session: the roadmap asks for a menu seen on screen, and the owner may open one by hand. Anything else Union cannot reach becomes a known gap and goes to the owner in Task 4: a tooltip's width, a size Qt fixes, a message's corners, frame or derived background (Task 1, (g)), a line some application draws in its own dialog.
- **Edited rules stay where they were.** Change values in place. Delete a declaration that reads the colour scheme only where a token replaces it. Delete from `:root` the `--tooltip-*` and `--popup-shadow-size` variables, `--dialog-shadow` if only a popup block used it, and the `--checkable-indicator-*` variables that no rule uses any more after the `menuitem` blocks change. Blocks outside this family keep their variables; the bars of P3.6 and the views of P3.7 are not touched.
- Files of the nested session and the probe stay in `tmp/` and are not committed, as in P2.3 to P3.7.

## Review Focus

- **A menu's divider drawn as a line.** Breeze's `menuseparator` is `height: 1px` with an inset. This is pinned in Task 3's spec (no `width` or `height` of `1px` in a popup block; `menuseparator` sets no colour but `transparent`) and in Task 4 by the case `menu`: `image colours` over its region `gap` lists only `popup`, the region is 8 px high, and `image frame --surface <popup>` exits 0 on the menu.
- **A strip between an open menu title and its menu, or between a combo box and its list.** Breeze's `menu` has `margin: var(--popup-shadow-size)`, which may set the menu 20 px away. This is pinned in Task 4 by the cases `menu-bar-open` and `combo-list`: `image colours` over their region `join`, the 2 px on each side of the line where the title or combo box meets the popup, lists only `popup` and the title's or combo box's own fill, and no `tone-0` or surface.
- **The transparent combo box list.** The owner saw a dialog through it. This is pinned in Task 4 by the case `combo-list`, whose window is `#ff00ff`: `image colours` over its region `list` lists no `#ff00ff`, and its items read as the gallery's.
- **An accent flash on a pressed menu item.** Breeze fills a pressed item with the highlight colour. This is pinned in Task 4 by the state `pressed` of `menu`, aliased to the gallery's `pressed`, which Task 2 draws as hovered: its fill is `tone-4`, and `image colours` over the item lists no `accent`.
- **A shadow over what the popup opens from.** A single `box-shadow` reaches above the popup's top edge, which D-67 cuts away. This is pinned in Task 4 by the case `menu-bar-open`: `image colours` over the open title's region `title` lists only `popup` and `text`, and the menu bar beside it only `tone-0` and `text`, with no darker tone than the bar's own.
