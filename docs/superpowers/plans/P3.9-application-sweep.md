# P3.9 Application Sweep Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Every window of Dolphin, Kate and Konsole that the owner can open is checked with Amber's colour scheme and Union style, and every breach of an anti-goal found in them (a rounded corner, a hairline, a cool grey) is fixed in the style or the colour scheme, or listed in the README as a known gap the owner accepted.

**Architecture:** P3.3 to P3.8 checked one widget family each, mostly in the probe. This plan checks whole windows instead. Task 1 writes the inventory: every window of the three applications, how it is opened in the nested session, and which regions of its shot belong to other parts. Task 2 shoots each window at rest, with the pointer on its controls, and inactive, reads each shot with `image greys` and `image frame`, and finds the cause of each breach with marker rules, as P3.3 to P3.8 did. Task 3 fixes, with a spec line each, the breaches that a rule of the style or a role of the colour scheme causes, among them the blocks no family plan restyled: the splitter, line frames, group boxes and dock widgets' title bars. Task 4 checks every window and every earlier probe case again, and the owner looks and decides on the gaps. Task 5 writes it down.

**Tech Stack:** Union CSS (`plasma-union` 6.8.2), Node 24 with `node:test`, the `image`, `browser`, `generate` and `tokens` tools, PySide6 6.11.2, the nested `kwin_wayland` session of P2.3 to P3.8 (`tmp/check-colour-scheme/`, with `in.sh`, `in-x11.sh` and `shot`), Dolphin, Kate 26.08.2, Konsole.

**Spec:** `docs/product/roadmap.md` (row P3.9; the notes under Step 3), `docs/product/vision.md` ("Anti-goals"; "What success looks like", its second line), `docs/architecture/design-language.md` (rule 3 "No lines"; "Surfaces"), `docs/architecture/platform.md` ("Union"; "Colour scheme", and its "Unverified" question for P3.9; "Konsole and Kate"), `README.md` ("Known gaps"), `docs/architecture/decisions.md` (D-25, D-94, D-95, D-98, D-108 to D-115), and the hand-offs to P3.9 in `docs/superpowers/plans/P3.6-bars.md` (the splitter; Dolphin's location bar and Kate's side tab bars), `P3.7-lists-and-tables.md` (what Dolphin draws itself) and `P3.8-popups.md` (a line above a dialog's buttons; the positive and neutral message backgrounds).

## Global Constraints

- Branch `feat/p3.9-application-sweep` from `master`. This plan file is committed on it.
- Use the glossary's words in British spelling. New in this plan, added to the glossary in Task 1 before any other file uses them: **splitter**, **group box**, **sweep**, **breach**. Never "handle" for a splitter, "divider" for anything but a hairline, or "frame" for a group box, except in Union's selectors (`splitter`, `frame`, `groupbox`, `dockwidget`, `titlebar`) and Qt's or KDE's class names (`QSplitter`, `QFrame::HLine`, `KSeparator`, `KMessageWidget`, `KUrlNavigator`).
- **Never change the owner's system settings.** `sha256sum ~/.config/kdeglobals ~/.config/kwinrc ~/.config/dolphinrc ~/.config/katerc ~/.config/konsolerc` gives the same result at the start (Task 1) and at the end (Task 5). Nothing is written under `~/.config`, `~/.local/share` or `/usr`. Every window is opened in the nested session, whose configuration lives in `tmp/check-colour-scheme/config/`. A change an application's own settings dialog makes (a Dolphin view mode, a Konsole profile) is made there only.
- **What a breach is.** In a window's shot, with the regions below left out: a cool grey that `image greys` reports; a line 1 or 2 px thick or a rounded corner that `image frame` reports, or that is seen by eye and confirmed with `image colours` along a strip across it. A round widget is not a breach. A tone that differs from the design language, but breaks no anti-goal, is not a breach: it is written down as **off** and offered to the owner in Task 4.
- **Left out of every check**, each named in the inventory for the shot it applies to, with its owner: the window decoration and the window outline (Step 5; the nested KWin draws Breeze's); Konsole's terminal area and Kate's terminal panel (P4.1); Kate's editor area and minimap (P4.2); icons, the text cursor and anything KWin draws (D-94); KWin's dimming behind a dialog (README); the Plasma shell's notifications (a non-goal). A breach that matches a README known gap is recorded with its decision's ID and not asked again.
- **How a breach is closed**, in this order:
  1. A rule of the Union style causes it: fix the rule, with a spec line in `tools/src/lib/union-style.spec.mjs` that fails before the fix.
  2. A role of the colour scheme causes it: fix its mapping in `parts/colour-scheme/roles.json` (or `tools/src/lib/colour-scheme.mjs` for a derived colour) with a spec line in `tools/src/lib/amber-colour-scheme.spec.mjs`, and regenerate with `node tools/src/bin/generate.mjs colour-scheme --write`; `node tools/src/bin/tokens.mjs pairs` passes.
  3. Neither reaches it (the application, Qt or KWin draws it): a known gap, which the owner accepts in Task 4.

  A fix that draws something the prototype does not show goes into `prototype/gallery.html` first, as P3.8's Task 2 did. A fix that only takes a line or a corner away needs no prototype change, since the prototype draws none. A fix keeps the specs of P3.2 to P3.8 passing and leaves their blocks alone unless the breach is in them.
- **The targets for blocks no family plan restyled** (my call, recorded as D-116 in Task 5). They apply only where a window of the inventory shows the block:

  | Block | Target |
  |---|---|
  | `splitter` | No fill and no line. Either it is invisible, its two sides meeting, or it is a gap at least 4 px wide that shows the tone behind it. Never 1 or 2 px of a tone that differs from both sides. Task 2 measures which widths Qt still lets the pointer drag. |
  | `frame[shape="horizontal-line"]`, `frame[shape="vertical-line"]` (`QFrame::HLine`, `KSeparator`) | Draws nothing: no fill, no border. Its space stays, as a gap. |
  | `frame[shape="panel"]`, `frame[shape="box"]` | No border, square. Its fill stays as P3.7 left it. |
  | `groupbox` | No frame and no fill of its own: it takes its surface. Its title is `text`. Square. |
  | `dockwidget`, `titlebar` (Kate's tool views, if they use them) | Take the tone of the surface they sit on, with `text`. No line between the title bar and the content. |
  | A dialog's button row | No line above it: a gap. If the application draws the line, it is a known gap. |
- **Contrast pairs.** A fix that puts text on a tone not already paired adds the pair in `prototype/tokens.css` in the same commit. `node tools/src/bin/tokens.mjs pairs` passes. No new token is expected; if one is needed it goes to the owner first.
- **Stop and ask the owner** when a breach could only be closed by changing a system setting, when a fix would change what Amber is (a new token, a new tone for a surface, dropping a rule another family needs), or when a breach in the main window of Dolphin, Kate or Konsole cannot be closed by the style or the colour scheme. Offer the shots and the cause found. Anything else out of reach goes to the owner together in Task 4.
- **An off finding** the owner asks to change in Task 4 is fixed in this plan if it is one rule with a spec line. Anything larger becomes a note in the roadmap under Step 3, for a plan of its own.
- **Edited rules stay where they were.** Change values in place, set each padding side on its own (P3.3), and delete a declaration that reads the colour scheme only where a token replaces it.
- Files of the nested session, the shots and the inventory stay in `tmp/` and are not committed, as in P2.3 to P3.8.

## Review Focus

- **A line only seen on hover.** A breadcrumb of Dolphin's location bar, a button of Kate's side tab bar or a Konsole tab may draw a frame only while hovered. Pinned in Task 2, Step 3: each main window's inventory row lists the controls the pointer rests on, and each gets its own shot and its own `image frame` run.
- **An inactive window.** Breeze's `.inactive` rules read `--window-inactive-border-color`, and Qt may switch to the colour scheme's Inactive colours, which nothing has checked. Pinned in Task 2, Step 3: each main window is shot a second time with another window focused, and both shots go through `image greys` and `image frame`.
- **A splitter drawn as a line.** Breeze's `splitter` is `width: 1px`. Between Dolphin's two split views, both tone 2, a splitter that shows tone 1 is a hairline. Pinned in Task 3 by the spec lines on `splitter`, and in Task 4 by `image colours` over a strip across Dolphin's split view, Kate's tool view edge and Konsole's split view, which lists no tone the two sides do not have.
- **A line inside a dialog.** KDE dialogs place `QFrame::HLine` and `KSeparator` between groups: Dolphin's Properties dialog, Kate's and Konsole's settings pages. Pinned in Task 3 by the spec lines on the line shapes, and in Task 4 by `image frame` over every settings page in the inventory.
- **A colour seen only in a rare state.** The positive and neutral message backgrounds have never been seen (platform.md, "Unverified"). Pinned in Task 2, Step 4: the inventory includes one message of each kind, and `image greys` runs on each.
