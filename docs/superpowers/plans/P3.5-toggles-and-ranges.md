# P3.5 Toggles and Ranges Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** The Union style draws check boxes, radio buttons, sliders and progress bars with the tones, marks, sizes and fills of the prototype's gallery in every state. This is checked by pixels in a Qt Widgets application and by eye in Dolphin.

**Architecture:** The toggle rules of `parts/union-style/amber/contents/css/check.css` and the range rules of `bars.css` are rewritten where they stand. They use only the variables of the generated `tokens.css` (D-97). The tick and the dot are the style's own SVG files, redrawn with square ends. P3.3's check is reused: `browser states` gives the gallery's expectation, the probe draws each state, and `image states` compares the two. A toggle's box and a slider's thumb are small parts of their widget, so the widget's most common colour is the surface around them. Two small extensions let the check read a part of a widget. `image states --region` reads only a box of each picture. `browser states --fill-property` and `--text-property` read a custom property of the gallery, such as `--mark` or `--thumb-tone`, in place of the background or the text colour. Task 1 first finds out what Union reaches on these widgets in Qt Widgets. Task 2 adds to the prototype the three things it does not yet show: a busy progress bar, a partly checked check box and a slider's tick marks.

**Tech Stack:** Union CSS (`plasma-union` 6.8.2), Node 24 with `node:test`, the `browser`, `image`, `generate` and `tokens` tools, PySide6 6.11.2, the nested `kwin_wayland` session of P2.3 to P3.4 (`tmp/check-colour-scheme/`), Dolphin.

**Spec:** `docs/product/roadmap.md` (row P3.5 and the notes under Step 3), `docs/architecture/design-language.md` ("Widgets": the toggles and ranges table, and the bullets on toggles, an unchecked toggle, ranges and thumbs; the widget tokens), `prototype/widgets.css` (the "Toggles" and "Ranges" rules), `prototype/gallery.html` (the rows `check-box`, `check-box-on`, `radio`, `radio-on`, `slider` and `progress`), `docs/architecture/platform.md` ("Union", "Union CSS", "Tools and switches"), `docs/architecture/decisions.md` (D-64, D-65, D-66, D-75, D-76, D-97, D-98, D-103, D-104), `docs/superpowers/plans/P3.4-text-inputs.md` (the probe, `--foot`, `--ignore`, and the outcome).

## Global Constraints

- Branch `feat/p3.5-toggles-and-ranges` from `master`. This plan file is committed on it.
- Use the glossary's words in British spelling: toggle, check box, radio button, round widget, slider, progress bar, track, fill, thumb, accent, at rest, hovered, focused, pressed, checked, disabled, tone, known gap. Never "handle", "knob", "groove" or "trough", except in Union's own selectors (`> handle`) and Qt's names (`SC_SliderHandle`). Use Qt class names only where the class matters.
- **Never change the owner's system settings.** `sha256sum ~/.config/kdeglobals ~/.config/kwinrc ~/.config/dolphinrc` gives the same result at the start (Task 1) and at the end (Task 6). Nothing is written under `~/.config`, `~/.local/share` or `/usr`. The style is read through the nested session's `XDG_DATA_DIRS` link, as in P3.1 to P3.4.
- **Anti-goals.** No rounded corner, no hairline and no cool grey. A radio button is a round widget and stays a circle: its `border-radius` keeps P3.2's `/* round widget */` mark. A dial is round too. The tick has square ends: no `round` cap or join in any SVG of the style. A slider's tick mark is a line unless it is at least 3 px wide, so tick marks are `var(--marker-width)` wide (below). No border and no outline on any widget of this family. Focus is shown by tone alone.
- **The target tones are the design language's toggles and ranges table**, as the prototype draws them:

  | Widget | At rest | Hovered | Focused | Pressed | Disabled |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Check box, radio button, unchecked: box / mark | `toggle` / none | `tone-1` / none | `toggle-focus` / none | as hovered | `toggle` / none |
  | Check box, radio button, checked: box / mark | `accent` / `on-accent` | `accent-hover` / `on-accent` | `accent-hover` / `on-accent` | as hovered | `toggle` / `text-dim` |
  | Toggle label | `text` | `text` | `text` | `text` | `text-dim` |
  | Slider: thumb / fill, on a `track` | `thumb` / `accent` | `thumb-hover` / `accent` | `raised-focus` / `accent` | `thumb-pressed` / `accent` | `tone-3` / `text-dim` |
  | Progress bar: track / fill | `track` / `accent` | — | — | — | `track` / `text-dim` |

  Precedence: pressed beats focused, and focused beats hovered. A disabled widget never changes. Within a block, put the state blocks in the order hovered, focused, pressed, disabled (P3.3: the later block wins). A toggle shows focus under `:visual-focus` only, as a button does: D-103 is about wells.
- **Sizes** (the prototype): a toggle's box is 16 × 16 px, with 8 px between the box and its label. The tick is two bars 2 px thick with square ends, inside a 5 × 9 px box turned 45°, as `.check-box::before` draws it. The dot is a circle of radius 3 px. A slider's track is 6 px; its thumb is 10 × 20 px (20 × 10 for a vertical slider), centred on the track. A progress bar is 6 px high and has no text. If Union cannot reach a size, record what it reaches and why in `platform.md` and keep the nearest size. This is not a reason to stop.
- **Decisions of this plan** (my call, recorded as D-105 in Task 6, and drawn in the prototype in Task 2 before the style):
  - **A partly checked check box** (`.indeterminate`) is drawn as a checked one, with an on-accent bar 8 × 2 px in the centre in place of the tick.
  - **A busy progress bar** moves a block of `accent` 32 px long along its `track`, left to right, over 1.5 s. Under `prefers-reduced-motion` the prototype shows it still, at the start of the track.
  - **A slider's tick marks** are `var(--marker-width)` (3 px) wide and 6 px long, in `text-dim`. They are `tone-3` when the slider is disabled. Tick marks have no accent: only the fill is.
  - `checkdelegate` and `radiodelegate` (Qt Quick) take the toggles' rules. `menuitem` keeps Breeze's rules until P3.8, but it takes the new tick's shape, since it shares the SVG file.
  - `switch` and `switchdelegate` (Qt Quick only) take the toggle's tones: the indicator is `toggle` unchecked and `accent` checked, and the handle is `thumb` unchecked and `on-accent` checked. They are not compared by pixels in this plan: the probe is Qt Widgets.
  - `dial` and `rangeslider` share the slider's block and take its tones. A dial stays round.
  - `.changed` keeps Breeze's rule, as in D-99 and D-101.
  - The inactive window changes no toggle and no range.
- **Contrast pairs.** Every pair this family uses must exist and pass: `on-accent` on `accent` and on `accent-hover`, `text-dim` on `toggle`, `accent` on `track` (3:1, a fill), `text-dim` on `track` and `text-dim` on `tone-2` (a tick mark, 3:1). `node tools/src/bin/tokens.mjs pairs` passes. A missing pair is added in `prototype/tokens.css` in Task 2.
- **Stop and ask the owner** before Task 4 if Task 1 finds that Union cannot colour a toggle's box in some state, or cannot draw a slider's thumb apart from its track. A toggle that looks the same checked and unchecked, or a slider with no visible thumb, changes what Amber is (CLAUDE.md, "Stop and ask"). Offer what Task 1 found. Anything else Union cannot reach becomes a known gap and is taken to the owner in Task 5: a disabled label drawn from the colour scheme, as with D-104; a hovered or pressed thumb that `render()` cannot reach and the screen does not show; text on a progress bar.
- **Edited rules stay where they were.** Change values in place. Delete a declaration that reads the colour scheme only where a token replaces it. Delete from `:root` the `--checkable-*`, `--switch-*`, `--bar-*` and `--dial-*` variables that no rule uses any more.

## Review Focus

- **The surface read as the box.** A check box widget is mostly its label and the surface. `image states` takes the most common colour as the fill, so it would compare the surface and pass every state, whatever colour the box has. This is pinned in Task 3: `--region` reads only the box. In Task 5, every toggle comparison names the box's region, which the probe writes into the manifest.
- **The tick drawn with round ends, or not drawn.** Breeze's `checkmark.svg` has `stroke-linecap="round"`, and Union draws a mask on or off per pixel. This is pinned in Task 4's spec: no SVG of the style holds `round`. In Task 5, `image colours` over the box of `check-box-on-rest` lists `on-accent`, and `check-box-on-disabled` lists `text-dim`.
- **A thumb that is hovered only when the pointer is on it.** Breeze styles `slider > handle:hovered`. The prototype changes the thumb whenever the pointer is anywhere over the slider. This is pinned in Task 5: the probe's `hovered` state puts the pointer over the track, away from the thumb, and the thumb must be `thumb-hover`.
- **Tick marks 2 px wide.** Breeze's `--bar-tickmark-thickness` is 2 px, which `image frame` reads as a line. The probe draws no tick marks by default. This is pinned in Task 5 by the case `slider-ticks` (`QSlider.TicksBelow`, interval 10): `image frame` exits 0 on it.
- **A progress bar that shows its text, or keeps Breeze's inset.** `QProgressBar` shows "40%" by default, and Breeze gives it `padding` and `inset` of 8 px at the sides, so the track is narrower than the widget. The probe hides the text. This is pinned in Task 5 by the case `progress-bar-text` (`setTextVisible(True)`): its fill and track span the widget's width, and either no text is drawn, or the text is recorded as a known gap.
