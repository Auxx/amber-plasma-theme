# P3.6 Bars Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** The Union style draws toolbars, the menu bar, the status bar, tab bars and scrollbars in the prototype's tones and sizes, in every state. This is checked by pixels in a Qt Widgets application and by eye in Dolphin and Kate.

**Architecture:** This plan rewrites, where they stand, the bar rules of `application.css` (`toolbar`), `popup.css` (`menubar`, `menubaritem`), `widgets.css` (`statusbar`), `tabs.css` (every block) and the `scrollbar` and `scrollareacorner` blocks of `bars.css` and `application.css`. They use only the variables of the generated `tokens.css` (D-97). The check is P3.3's to P3.5's: `browser states` gives the gallery's expectation, the probe draws each state, and `image states --region` compares one part of a widget. Bars are mostly surface, so every comparison of a tab, a menu title or a thumb names the region that the probe writes into the manifest. Task 1 first finds out what Union reaches on these widgets in Qt Widgets. Task 2 adds to the prototype what it does not show yet: a menu bar, a tab's close button and a disabled thumb. It also adds the glossary's new terms.

**Tech Stack:** Union CSS (`plasma-union` 6.8.2), Node 24 with `node:test`, the `browser`, `image`, `generate` and `tokens` tools, PySide6 6.11.2, the nested `kwin_wayland` session of P2.3 to P3.5 (`tmp/check-colour-scheme/`), Dolphin, Kate.

**Spec:** `docs/product/roadmap.md` (row P3.6 and the notes under Step 3), `docs/architecture/design-language.md` ("Surfaces"; "Widgets": the tool button row, the scrollbar thumb row, the tab row, and the bullets on selection, thumbs and scrollbars; the widget tokens), `prototype/widgets.css` (the "Navigation" rules), `prototype/scrollbars.css`, `prototype/shell.css` (`.toolbar`, `.status`), `prototype/gallery.html` (the rows `tool-button`, `scrollbar` and `tab`), `docs/architecture/platform.md` ("Union", "Union CSS", "Tools and switches"), `docs/architecture/decisions.md` (D-21, D-64 to D-68, D-95, D-97, D-98, D-103, D-105), `docs/superpowers/plans/P3.5-toggles-and-ranges.md` (the probe, `--region`, `--fill-property`, and the outcome).

## Global Constraints

- Branch `feat/p3.6-bars` from `master`. This plan file is committed on it.
- Use the glossary's words in British spelling: toolbar, menu bar, menu title (new, Task 2), status bar, tab bar, tab, scrollbar, thumb, track, block marker, surface, tone, at rest, hovered, focused, pressed, selected, disabled, inactive, known gap. Never "handle", "groove" or "trough", except in Union's own selectors (`> handle`, `menubaritem`) and Qt's names (`SC_ScrollBarSlider`).
- **Never change the owner's system settings.** `sha256sum ~/.config/kdeglobals ~/.config/kwinrc ~/.config/dolphinrc ~/.config/katerc` gives the same result at the start (Task 1) and at the end (Task 5). Nothing is written under `~/.config`, `~/.local/share` or `/usr`. Showing Dolphin's menu bar (Ctrl+M) or opening tabs is done in the nested session, whose configuration lives in `tmp/check-colour-scheme/config/`.
- **Anti-goals.** No rounded corner, no hairline and no cool grey. Every `border-radius` of the bar blocks is `0px`; Breeze's `.first` and `.last` corner rules are deleted. A toolbar's separator is an 8 px gap with no fill, as a menu separator is: not a 1 px line. A toolbar's grip (`toolbar > handle`) has no fill. The splitter is not touched here (P3.9). The only borders allowed are the selected tab's block marker (below) and P3.4's focus bar.
- **The target tones** are the design language's, as the prototype draws them:

  | Widget | At rest | Hovered | Focused | Pressed / open | Selected | Disabled |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Toolbar (header) | `tone-0` | — | — | — | — | — |
  | Menu bar | `tone-0` | — | — | — | — | — |
  | Menu title (new, my call) | no fill / `text` | `tone-3` / `text` | as hovered | open: `popup` / `text` | — | no fill / `text-dim` |
  | Status bar | `tone-1` / `text-dim` | — | — | — | — | — |
  | Tab bar | `tone-0` | — | — | — | — | — |
  | Tab | no fill / `text` | `tone-1` / `text` | `surface-focus` / `text` | — | `tone-2` / `text`, marker `accent` | no fill / `text-dim` |
  | Selected tab | — | as selected | `surface-focus` / `text`, marker | — | — | — |
  | Tab's close button (new, my call) | no fill / `text-dim` | `danger` / `on-danger` | — | `danger` / `on-danger` | — | no fill / `text-dim` |
  | Page of a tab widget | `tone-2` | — | — | — | — | — |
  | Scrollbar: track / thumb | none / `thumb` | none / `thumb-hover` | — | none / `thumb-pressed` | — | none / `tone-3` (new, my call) |

  Precedence: pressed beats focused, and focused beats hovered. A disabled widget never changes. Within a block, the state blocks come in the order hovered, focused, pressed, checked or selected, disabled (P3.3: the later block wins), except that a selected tab's `:hovered` changes nothing.
- **Sizes** (the prototype): a scrollbar is 12 px across, its thumb the full 12 px, with no arrow buttons and no padding. A selected tab's block marker is `var(--marker-width)` (3 px) deep, along its whole outer edge. A tab's padding is 8 px at the sides and 6 px at the top and bottom, as `.tab` has. The header toolbar keeps Breeze's height. If Union cannot reach a size, record what it reaches and why in `platform.md` and keep the nearest size. This alone is not a reason to stop.
- **Decisions of this plan** (my call, recorded as D-108 in Task 5, and drawn in the prototype in Task 2 before the style):
  - **A menu title** is drawn as a tool button on a tone 0 toolbar when hovered (`tone-3`). The keyboard highlight is the hovered tone, as for a menu item, since Qt does not tell the two apart. Open, it takes `popup`, so that it joins the menu below it, as a selected tab joins its page.
  - **A tab's close button** is dim text at rest, and `danger` with an `on-danger` glyph when hovered or pressed, as the window's close button is. The design language has no darker danger token, so pressed stays `danger`.
  - **The block marker sits on the tab's outer edge**: the top for a tab bar above its pages, the bottom for one below, the left or the right for one at the side (Kate's `kmultitabbar`). It is drawn as a `border-<edge>` in `accent`, set at rest in `transparent`, because Union draws only a border that exists at rest (P3.4).
  - **A disabled scrollbar thumb** is `tone-3`, as a disabled slider's thumb is.
  - **The corner between two scrollbars** (`scrollareacorner`) is transparent, so that it takes the view's tone.
  - **Only a header toolbar is tone 0**: one with `.header` or `[colorset="header"]`, or the first toolbar of a main window if that is all Union tells apart (Task 1). Any other toolbar has no fill and takes its surface. `[colorset="complementary"]` follows the header.
  - `tabbutton.immutable` and `tabbar.immutable` (Kirigami) take the tab's rules. A non-interactive scrollbar (Qt Quick) takes the thumb's tones at Breeze's width.
  - A scrollbar's thumb takes no focused tone in Qt Widgets: a focused scroll area's tint, and the thumb's dim text on it, belong to P3.7's views.
  - The inactive window changes no bar.
- **Contrast pairs.** Every pair this family uses must exist and pass: `text` on `tone-0`, `tone-1`, `tone-2`, `tone-3`, `popup` and `surface-focus`; `text-dim` on `tone-0` and `tone-1`; `on-danger` on `danger`; `accent` on `tone-2` and `surface-focus` (the marker, 3:1); `thumb` on `tone-2`. `node tools/src/bin/tokens.mjs pairs` passes. A missing pair is added in `prototype/tokens.css` in Task 2. No new token is expected. If one is needed, it goes to the owner first.
- **Stop and ask the owner** before Task 3 if Task 1 finds that Union cannot tell a selected tab apart from the others, or cannot draw a mark of 3 px on any edge of it, or cannot draw a scrollbar's thumb apart from its track. A tab bar with no visible selected tab changes what Amber is (CLAUDE.md, "Stop and ask"). Offer what Task 1 found. Anything else Union cannot reach becomes a known gap and goes to the owner in Task 4: the status bar's text drawn from the colour scheme; a menu title that `render()` cannot show open and the screen does not show either; Qt's arrow buttons on a scrollbar; a toolbar's separator that Qt draws itself.
- **Edited rules stay where they were.** Change values in place. Delete a declaration that reads the colour scheme only where a token replaces it. Delete from `:root` the `--tab-*`, `--bar-*`, `--header-*`, `--complementary-*` and `--window-*-border-color` variables that no rule uses any more. Blocks outside this family keep their variables.
- Files of the nested session and the probe stay in `tmp/` and are not committed, as in P2.3 to P3.5.

## Review Focus

- **The bar read as the tab.** A tab bar is mostly its own surface, so `image states` without a region would compare `tone-0` and pass every state. This is pinned in Task 4: the probe writes each tab's region (`QTabBar.tabRect(i)`) into the manifest, and every tab comparison names it.
- **The marker on the wrong edge, or as a line.** Breeze sets the selected tab's border on `[direction]`. A bar below its pages, or one at the side, can carry the marker on the edge that touches the page, or a 1 px leftover. This is pinned in Task 4 by the cases `tab-bar-south` (`QTabBar.RoundedSouth`) and `tab-bar-west` (`RoundedWest`). `image colours` over the outer 3 px of the selected tab lists only `accent`, over the inner 3 px lists no `accent`, and `image frame` exits 0.
- **A thumb narrower than its bar, or an arrow left at each end.** Breeze's `scrollbar` is 20 px with `padding: var(--medium-spacing)`, so its thumb is narrow and centred, and Qt Widgets may still draw arrow buttons. This is pinned in Task 4: the probe's `scroll-bar` and `scroll-bar-vertical` cases have a thumb region exactly 12 px across, and `image colours` over the bar's ends lists only the probe's surface.
- **A toolbar's separator drawn as a 1 px line.** `toolbar > separator` is `width: 1px` in Breeze. This is pinned in Task 3's spec (no `width` or `height` of `1px` in a bar block) and in Task 4 by the `tool-bar` case, which has a separator: `image frame` exits 0 on it.
- **A tone 1 square in the corner of a tone 2 view.** `scrollareacorner` is the Window set's background, which is tone 1 since D-95. This is pinned in Task 4 by the case `scroll-area-both` (a `QScrollArea` with both scrollbars shown): `image colours` over the corner's region lists only the view's `tone-2`.
