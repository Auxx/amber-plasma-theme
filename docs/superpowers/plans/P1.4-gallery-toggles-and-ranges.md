# P1.4 Gallery: toggles and ranges Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** The gallery page `prototype/gallery.html` gains two families: "Toggles" (check boxes and radio buttons, off and on) and "Ranges" (sliders, progress bars and scrollbars). Their states sit side by side, and each control reacts to the pointer and the keyboard. Beside them are candidates for the focused toggle (O-3), for the unchecked toggle and for the track (O-1), each with its measured contrast.

**Architecture:** P1.3's pattern continues. The new tones become tokens with contrast pairs. `widgets.css` writes every state rule for the pseudo-class and its forced class together. `prototype-gallery.spec.mjs` checks every state's tones in Chrome, and checks that a live control matches its forced copy. Check boxes and radio buttons are native inputs with `appearance: none`. A check box draws its tick in `::before`, and a radio button draws its dot as a `radial-gradient` on itself. Both take the mark's colour from a custom property, `--mark`. A slider is a native range input. Its thumb and fill take their colours from `--thumb` and `--fill`, and its fill length from `--value`. One small script, `gallery.js`, copies a slider's value into `--value` while it is dragged. The anti-goal check learns to allow that script and to read it. Scrollbars are drawn twice. Divs show the forced states. The real scrollbars of the page and of a scroll area use `::-webkit-scrollbar-*` in the same rules as the divs.

**Tech Stack:** HTML and CSS opened as `file://` (D-35), plus one script file; Node 24 ES modules, `node:test`, `node:assert/strict`; the existing `tokens.mjs`, `prototype-check.mjs` and `browser.mjs` (playwright-core with the installed Chrome, D-46). No new dependency.

**Spec:** `docs/product/roadmap.md` (P1.4, and the paragraph under the Step 1 table), `docs/architecture/design-language.md` (Principles, Widgets), `docs/architecture/accessibility.md` (rules 1, 2, 4; "Where Amber departs from WCAG on purpose"), `docs/architecture/decisions.md` (D-2, D-4, D-35, D-36, D-47, O-1, O-3), `docs/product/glossary.md` (Round widget, Widget states), and `docs/superpowers/plans/P1.3-gallery-buttons-and-inputs.md` for the gallery's markup, its spec and its candidates.

## Global Constraints

- Use the glossary's words and British spelling. The new terms go into the glossary first (Task 1, Step 1).
- Every constraint of P1.3 still holds: no rounded corner, no hairline and no cool grey; every colour from `prototype/tokens.css` except in a candidate's `style`; no `<style>` and no `<hr>`; a `style` attribute only sets custom properties; controls reset with `appearance: none`, `border: 0`, `border-radius: 0`, `outline: none` and `font: inherit`. ## Review Focus

- The live slider: a fill that does not follow the thumb when it is dragged or moved with the arrow keys, or a `--value` in the HTML that disagrees with `value`. Pinned in Task 4 (`moves the fill with the value`, `starts each fill at its value`).
- Chrome's own drawing leaking in: the native tick, the circle and focus ring of a radio, the rounded track and thumb of a range input, and the grey page scrollbar of a page that is now longer than the window. Pinned by `breaks no anti-goal in any state` (Tasks 3 to 5), by `appearance` read as `none` on every toggle and slider (Tasks 3 and 4), and by `styles the real scrollbars as the drawn ones` (Task 5).
- Pointing at a toggle's label rather than its box. The box should show its hovered tone, and a click should flip it. Pinned in Task 3 (`hovers the box from its label`).
- The keyboard: Tab reaches every toggle and slider that is not disabled, and none of the progress bars or drawn scrollbars. A slider's arrow keys move it. Pinned by the extended `takes the keyboard in order` (Task 3) and by `moves the fill with the value` (Task 4).
- A script that grows into a second source of colour or state, for example by setting a class or a colour. Pinned by `scriptOffences` (Task 2).

## Deviations

Found while doing the plan (my call):

- **Toggle state rules carry `:enabled` and `:checked` or `:not(:checked)`.** All the rules then weigh the same. File order gives focused over hovered, and a disabled toggle needs no override.
- **The `browser` tool gains `shot --scrollbars`.** Headless Chrome hides scrollbars, so the real ones could not be seen. `openPage` takes `scrollbars: true`, and two specs pin it. A one-off script would have broken the `ai-tools` rule.
- **A test that names colours as tokens** now also reads `scroll-thumb` for `#3c3832`, which is the true alias list.
- **Layout went into other files.** The `.live` layout rules are in `widgets.css`. The `.vignette.is-stacked` rule (a slider over a progress bar) is in `gallery.css`.
- **Seen by eye:** a pressed slider thumb (tone 1) nearly vanishes against a tone 0 track. It is kept as planned and noted in the design language for the owner's review in P1.7.
