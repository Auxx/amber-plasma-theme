# P3.4 Text Inputs Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** The Union style draws text fields, spin boxes, text areas and editable combo boxes as wells: the tones, text colours, focus bar and heights of the prototype's gallery in every state. This is checked by pixels in a Qt Widgets application and by eye in Dolphin.

**Architecture:** The well rules of `parts/union-style/amber/contents/css/text.css`, and the `.editable` block of `button.css`, are rewritten where they stand, using only the variables of the generated `tokens.css` (D-97). P3.3's check is reused: `browser states` gives the gallery's expectation of each state, the probe draws each state, and `image states` compares the two. Two small extensions make it read a well. `browser states` takes a placeholder's colour as the text of an empty field. `image states` leaves the focus bar out of the fill and text and checks it on its own. Task 1 first finds out how Union can draw the focus bar, a 3 px accent band at the foot of a focused well. P3.2 removed every border, and Union draws no `outline` and no gradient in Qt Widgets. The bar is the one part of this family the platform may refuse.

**Tech Stack:** Union CSS (`plasma-union` 6.8.2), Node 24 with `node:test`, the `browser`, `image`, `generate` and `tokens` tools, PySide6 6.11.2, the nested `kwin_wayland` session of P2.3 to P3.3 (`tmp/check-colour-scheme/`), Dolphin.

**Spec:** `docs/product/roadmap.md` (row P3.4 and the notes under Step 3), `docs/architecture/design-language.md` ("Widgets": the first table, and the bullets on wells, a focused well, an empty well and precedence), `prototype/widgets.css` (the "Wells" rules and their states) and `prototype/gallery.html` (the rows `text-field`, `spin` and `text-area`), `docs/architecture/colour-scheme.md` ("A well is not the View set"), `docs/architecture/platform.md` ("Union", "Union CSS", "Tools and switches", and the colour-scheme findings on the text cursor and on text fields), `docs/architecture/decisions.md` (D-64, D-69, D-75, D-94, D-97, D-98, D-99), `docs/superpowers/plans/P3.3-buttons.md` (the probe, the two `states` commands and the outcome).

- **Never change the owner's system settings.** `sha256sum ~/.config/kdeglobals ~/.config/kwinrc ~/.config/dolphinrc` gives the same result at the start (Task 1) and at the end (Task 5). Nothing is written under `~/.config`, `~/.local/share` or `/usr`. The style is read through the nested session's `XDG_DATA_DIRS` link, as in P3.1 to P3.3.
- **Anti-goals.** No rounded corner, no hairline and no cool grey. The focus bar is 3 px high and is not a hairline (glossary; `image frame` counts 3 px as a band). It is the only edge a well may draw. P3.2's spec forbids every border. If the bar is drawn as a border, the spec allows exactly that one declaration, `border-bottom` of `var(--well-focus-edge-width)` in `var(--well-focus-edge)` inside a `:visual-focus` block of a well, and nothing else. Focus is never shown by a line or an outline.
- **The target tones are the design language's first table**, as the prototype draws them:

  | Widget | At rest | Hovered | Focused | Disabled |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Text field, spin box, text area, editable combo box | `well` / `text` | `tone-1` / `text` | `well-focus` / `text`, with the focus bar | `well` / `text-dim` |

  A placeholder is `text-dim`, and a disabled placeholder too. A text selection is `on-accent` on `accent`. Precedence: focused beats hovered, so a hovered, focused well is `well-focus` with the bar. A disabled well never changes. A well has no pressed tone.
- **The spin box's step buttons** (the prototype's `.spin-step`): no fill and a `text-dim` glyph at rest; `tone-3` / `text` hovered; `tone-2` pressed; no fill / `text-dim` disabled. They take no keyboard focus and stay clear of the focus bar, so the bar runs the whole width of the well and the steps end 3 px above its foot.
- **Heights** (the prototype: `height: 30px`; `padding: 0 8px` for a text field and a spin box's field, `6px 8px` for a text area): a text field, a spin box and an editable combo box are 30 px high at scale 1, with 8 px at the sides. Since P3.3 found that Union reads a two-value `padding` swapped, set each side alone. A text area has 6 px at the top and the bottom. If a widget cannot be made 30 px high, record the height and the reason in `platform.md`, as P3.3 did. Keep the nearest height that can be reached. This is not a reason to stop.
- **Decisions of this plan** (my call, recorded as D-101 in Task 5):
  - The editable combo box is a well (D-99). Its arrow is `text`, as the read-only combo box's is. Open, Union marks it `:checked` (P3.3), and it keeps its tone and text.
  - `searchfield` (Qt Quick only) takes the text field's rules.
  - `textfield.changed`, `textarea.changed` and `spinbox.changed` keep Breeze's rule, as `button.changed` did (D-99).
  - `textfield.inside-table`, the editor of a table cell, is left to P3.7, and its colours are not touched here.
  - The inactive window changes no well.
- **The focus bar.** Stop and ask the owner before Task 3 if Task 1 finds no way to draw a 3 px accent band at the foot of a focused well in Qt Widgets, or finds only one that moves the text when focus arrives. A focused well would otherwise look like a hovered one. That changes what Amber looks like (CLAUDE.md, "Stop and ask"). Offer the answers Task 1 found, and as a fallback: focus shown by tone alone, with the bar listed as a known gap.
- **The text cursor.** Qt draws it inverted, which gives a cool grey on every tone (P2.3). D-94 counts it as outside Amber. If Task 1 finds no Union CSS property that colours it, it stays as it is and goes into the README's known gaps. This is not a reason to stop.
- **Edited rules stay where they were.** Change values in place, and delete declarations that read the colour scheme only where a token replaces them. Within a block, put the state blocks in the order hovered, focused, disabled (P3.3: the later block wins).

## Review Focus

- **The focus bar read as the text.** `image states` takes as the text the colour farthest from the fill. On a tone 1 focused well, the accent bar is farther than the `text-dim` placeholder, so every focused state would fail, or would pass for the wrong reason. This is pinned in Task 2: `--foot` leaves the bottom rows out of the fill and text and checks them on their own. The test uses a synthetic well with a 3 px accent foot and dim text.
- **The cursor read as the text.** In a focused field, a 1 px cursor in an inverted light grey is the colour farthest from the fill, as the bar is. This is pinned in Task 1, Step 5, which finds out whether `render()` draws the cursor. If it does, Task 2's `--ignore <hex>` leaves the cursor's colour out, and Task 4 reads it on its own with `image greys`.
- **A placeholder expected as text.** The gallery's empty fields show a placeholder, but `getComputedStyle(input).color` is `text`. Read naïvely, the expectation says `text` where the well shows `text-dim`. This is pinned in Task 2, Step 1: `browser states` reads the `::placeholder` colour for an empty input or text area that has a placeholder, and the probe's fields are empty with a placeholder in every state but `filled`.
- **A text area whose viewport the style never reaches.** `QTextEdit` paints its viewport from the palette, not from the style. The text area may stay tone 2 (the View set) whatever `textarea` says, as P2.3 saw in Dolphin. This is pinned in Task 1, Step 4, and in Task 4, Step 2 by `image states --case text-edit`. If the viewport cannot be reached, the gap goes to the owner with Task 4's look.
- **The bar under the step buttons, or the steps over the bar.** In a focused spin box, the step buttons are drawn over the well's foot and may cover the bar's right end, or push the bar inwards. This is pinned in Task 4, Step 3: the bottom 3 rows of `spin-box-focused` are accent across their whole width.

**Interfaces:**
- Consumes: `states` of `browser.mjs`, and `fill` and the `states` command of `image.mjs` (P3.3).
- Produces:
  - `browser states`: when the widget is an `input` or `textarea` whose value is empty and that has a `placeholder`, `text` is the computed `color` of its `::placeholder`. Nothing else changes.
  - `fill(image, { surface, foot = 0, ignore = [] })`: the bottom `foot` rows are left out of `background` and `text`, and so are pixels within ±1 a channel of a hex in `ignore`. The return gains `foot: hex | null`, the most common colour of those rows, or `null` when `foot` is 0.
  - `image states … [--foot <px>] [--bar <hex>] [--bar-states <state,...>] [--ignore <hex>]...`: with `--foot`, a state named in `--bar-states` passes only when its foot is `--bar` (±1), and every other state only when its foot equals its own fill. A foot mismatch prints `foot` and both hexes with their tokens. `--bar` without `--foot`, or `--bar-states` without `--bar`, exits 2.

## Outcome — 2026-11-12

Done. The probe's text field, spin box and editable combo box match the
gallery in every state, the combined states, a clicked field and the focus
bar included; `image frame` finds no rounded corner or line on any of them.
Dolphin's filter bar, location bar and settings dialog show only tokens,
and their only greys are icons (D-94). The owner tried the wells in Dolphin
and found them right after two changes. The owner's settings files kept
their sums. What differed from the plan:

- **The bar was taken away by a later file.** Breeze's `application.css`,
  imported after `text.css`, has its own `:visual-focus` rule for
  `textfield` and `textarea`; they were taken out of it.
- **A text area is not a well** in Qt Widgets: its viewport is the View
  set, tone 2, which no rule reaches. Its frame carries the bar, as a band
  in tone 2 at rest, since a border set only on focus is not drawn. The
  owner accepted it as a known gap (D-104).
- **A spin box's steps cannot be styled** in Qt Widgets, and disabled text
  and placeholders come from the colour scheme: known gaps the owner
  accepted (D-104). The text cursor is listed with them (D-94).
- **The owner changed two things on seeing Dolphin.** A hovered well was
  tone 1, a dialog's body, and vanished there: it is `well-hover` now,
  accent 14 % over tone 0 (D-102), in the prototype too. A clicked field
  showed no focus, since `:visual-focus` is keyboard focus only: a well
  shows focus under `:active-focus` too (D-103).
- **Heights.** A text field needed 5 px above and below its 20 px line; a
  spin box's height came from its two 16 px steps, now 15 px each.
- **Tools.** `fill` gives `foot` only when asked for. The probe gained
  `--no-cursor`, since the cursor is drawn inverted over the placeholder and
  `--ignore` cannot take out its blends. `image frame` read a focus bar's
  corners as rounded, because accent lies nearer magenta than tone 1; a
  corner is now compared with its sides' colour too.
