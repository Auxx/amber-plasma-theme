# P0.5 Spike: glow without a shadow Implementation Plan

**Goal:** Find out whether a glow can be shown on a button in a Qt Widgets application without `box-shadow`, which Union 6.8.2 does not draw there (spike P0.3), and make pictures of the result for the owner.

**Asked for by:** the owner, on 2026-11-11, before deciding open question O-2.

**Spec:** `docs/architecture/platform.md` (finding P0.3 and the "Unverified" table), `docs/architecture/design-language.md` ("Glow and shadow"), `docs/architecture/decisions.md` (O-2).

**Approach:** The same throwaway set-up as P0.3: a style `amber-spike` (a copy of the Breeze style plus one file of rules), installed for the user, used by one PySide6 test application through `UNION_STYLE_NAME`. The application gains a `--hover <name>` option that marks a button as under the pointer, so that a hovered button can be pictured without a pointer.

## Global Constraints

- As in P0.3: no system setting is changed, nothing is written under `/usr`, all spike files live in `tmp/spike-glow/` and are not committed, the style is uninstalled at the end.
- Glow values come from the design language: accent `rgb(209, 176, 114)`; hovered 14 px blur at 28 % alpha; focused 18 px blur at 45 % alpha.
- The anti-goals hold for the result: whatever makes the glow must not read as a hairline and must not round a corner.
- The pictures are kept in `tmp/spike-glow/shots/` for the owner. The owner decides O-2; this plan does not.

## Tasks

- [x] **1. Rebuild the test application and the style** as in P0.3, with `--hover`.
- [x] **2. Learn what the Qt Widgets style does with each property**, one loud rule at a time, a picture each:
  - `inset` with a negative value: is the background drawn outside the widget?
  - `inset` with a positive value, and `margin`: where is the background drawn, and does the widget grow to keep its content's size?
  - `background-image` with a PNG and with an SVG: is it drawn with `background-color` or in its place, is it stretched, tiled or drawn at its own size, does `inset` move it, and what do `background-image-width`, `-height` and `-offset-*` do?
- [x] **3. Build the best glow those answers allow** for `button:hovered` and `button:visual-focus`, at the design language's amounts, with no glow at rest. Picture: at rest, hovered, focused; on the buttons `free`, `touch-1` and `edge`; and on a button much wider than the others, to show whether the glow keeps its width.
- [x] **4. Measure the cost:** how much larger a button or the gap between buttons becomes, compared with the same style without the glow.
- [x] **5. Uninstall, delete the spike's files except the pictures, and write the finding** into `docs/architecture/platform.md` ("Spike findings", and the "Unverified" row this answers). Set the roadmap row. Commit.
- [x] **6. Show the owner the pictures** and what the glow costs. Stop there.
