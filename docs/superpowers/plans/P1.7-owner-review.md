# P1.7 Owner review Plan

> **For agentic workers:** this plan is a review held with the owner, not a build. Do it in the session the owner is in, task by task (superpowers:executing-plans). Do not hand a task to a subagent: every task waits for the owner's words. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** The owner looks at the three pages of the prototype, says what is off, and chooses among the candidates. The tokens are tuned to what the owner said, the open questions O-1, O-3 and O-4 are closed as decisions of the owner, O-5 is closed or handed to P4.1 by the owner, and the owner approves the prototype.

**Architecture:** One task per page, in the order window shell, gallery, samples, then a closing task. On each page the tones and the accent are looked at before the candidates, because every candidate is measured against the tones. Each task is a loop: the owner looks and reacts; the remark becomes a change of tokens, tried with `tokens try` before it is written; the tests, the contrast pairs and the rendered anti-goal check run; the owner reloads and compares. When the owner chooses a candidate, its value becomes the token, the provisional minimum of its pairs becomes a settled one, the candidate group leaves the page, and the documents change in the same commit.

**Tech Stack:** The prototype as it is (HTML and CSS opened as `file://`, D-35); the `tokens`, `colour` and `browser` tools. No new dependency and no new page.

**Spec:** `docs/product/roadmap.md` (P1.7: "The owner approves the prototype and O-1, O-3 and O-4 are closed in the decisions"), `docs/architecture/decisions.md` (O-1, O-3, O-4, O-5), `docs/architecture/accessibility.md` (rules 3 to 5, the provisional minimums, "First measurements"), `docs/architecture/design-language.md` (tones, states, the widgets' tokens, the window outline, glow and shadow, the terminal colours and editor theme), `docs/product/vision.md` (anti-goals; what success looks like).

## Global Constraints

- Branch `feat/p1.7-owner-review`. This file is committed on it.
- Use the glossary's words and British spelling.
- **The owner decides; the designer measures.** Nothing in this plan is chosen for the owner. A row added to the decisions for O-1, O-3, O-4 or O-5 is marked Owner and carries the owner's reason in the owner's sense. A value the owner did not speak about but which had to move with a choice is a separate row, marked Designer, and is named to the owner before the commit.
- **The owner looks at the real page**, at 100 % zoom on their own monitor, opened from disk. A screenshot from the `browser` tool is for the designer's own check and for the record, not a stand-in for the owner's eye.
- **Never change the owner's system settings**, and write nothing under `/usr`.
- **The anti-goals are hard failures:** no rounded corner, no hairline but the window outline, no cool grey. `browser check` runs on all three pages after every change of tokens.
- **A change is tried before it is written.** `tokens try '<token>=<value>'` shows which pairs change. A change that makes a pair fail or become close goes back to the owner with the numbers and with the nearest values that pass (`colour solve`); it is not written quietly, and no minimum is lowered to make it pass without the owner saying so.
- **Every colour comes from `prototype/tokens.css`.** A tuned value is changed there and nowhere else; a mix is written in the token's comment, as now.
- **When a candidate is chosen** (my call):
  - its value becomes the token's value, so that the main view of the page shows it;
  - its candidate group is removed from the page, and the counts in `prototype-candidates.spec.mjs`, `prototype-gallery.spec.mjs` and `prototype-samples.spec.mjs` follow. A candidate is not a token until it is chosen; after the choice the others are history, and git keeps them;
  - the pairs that had a provisional minimum get the minimum that the owner's choice implies, and "provisional" leaves `contrast-pairs.json`, the accessibility document and the design language for those pairs;
  - if the owner wants none of the candidates shown, new ones are added to the group first, each with its measured contrast, and the owner looks again.
- **A remark that would change what Amber is** (a border after all, a rounded corner, a second accent, a glow on a widget) is not tuned in: stop, and talk it through with the owner as a change to the vision.
- **A remark about something Union or Aurorae may not draw** is checked against `docs/architecture/platform.md` before it is tuned in, so that the prototype does not promise it.
- Commits are small: one per round of remarks that leaves the tests green, and one per closed question.

## What the owner is asked on each page

These are prompts for the designer to put to the owner, not a form. The owner's free reaction comes first; the prompts catch what was not mentioned.

**Window shell (`prototype/index.html`)**

- Are the title bar, toolbar, sidebar, content area and status area told apart by tone alone? Is any pair too close, or too far apart?
- Do the surfaces read as warm stone, with no cool grey? Is the accent the right gold?
- Is the text comfortable to read, and is dim text dim enough without being hard?
- The selected item with its block marker; the hovered item; the window buttons at rest, hovered and pressed; the close button's danger.
- Active against inactive: can the window in use be told at a glance?
- **O-4, window outline:** which colour and which width, for the active and for the inactive window? Can the two windows be told apart where they overlap?
- **O-4, window glow:** how wide and how strong? **Shadow:** how wide and how strong for the inactive window?
- **O-4, what is measured:** is the outline held to its minimum against the bare tones only, or against the glow too? (The starting outline measures 1.14:1 against the glow over tone 0.)

**Gallery (`prototype/gallery.html`)**

- Each family in turn, by eye, pointer and keyboard (Tab through it): buttons, inputs, toggles, ranges, navigation, views, popups, the dialog. Is each state told from the one before it?
- Disabled widgets: clearly off, still readable?
- **O-1, empty well:** a darker well, a lighter one, or a well edge? **Unchecked toggle** and **track:** which tone?
- **O-3, focused:** how strong is the tint of a focused raised widget, a focused well and a focused toggle? Does the primary button, and does a checked toggle, need a focused tone of its own, different from hovered?
- **Selected item** (not an open question, but shown as candidates): with or without the block marker?
- **Popup:** is a menu acceptable without a shadow, should Union not draw one (P3.8)?

**Samples (`prototype/samples.html`)**

- Do the ANSI colours read as one muted, warm family? Are red, green, yellow and blue told apart at a glance, and the normal row from the bright one?
- The syntax colours on the code: is any kind of code too loud or too quiet?
- The gutter, the current line and the text selection.
- **Terminal background:** tone 2 as the frame, or darker?
- **Text selection:** which candidate?
- **O-5:** accept light text on ANSI backgrounds as a known gap, darken the normal colours, or leave it to P4.1?

## Review Focus

- **A choice of the designer's passed off as the owner's.** Each row of the decisions that this plan marks Owner can be traced to a line of the review log.
- **A minimum lowered to fit a value.** A settled minimum comes from the owner's choice of what a cue must do, not from whatever the chosen colour happens to measure.
- **A caption that no longer matches its figure.** After a tone changes, every ratio printed on the pages is measured again; `prototype-candidates.spec.mjs` pins them.
- **A candidate left behind.** A removed group leaves no rule, token or spec count that only it used.
- **A document that still speaks of P1.7 as to come.**

## Deviations

Found while doing the plan (my call):

- **The review needed new candidates more than the old ones.** The
  shadow, the glow, the focused well and the normal ANSI colours were all
  chosen from candidates added during the review, not from those of P1.2
  to P1.6.
- **Two things were tried in the tokens before they were decided**, at
  the owner's request: the focused well (uncommitted, with two pairs
  failing) and the normal ANSI colours (committed, with their minimum
  lowered to 3 for the trial).
- **New mechanisms in the prototype:** an eased fade and insets for a
  window's light (`window.css`), the focus bar, `--surface-focus`, the
  thumb tokens, and ANSI colours as backgrounds in the terminal sample.
- **The check learnt one rule:** a candidate's `--ansi-*` override may be
  any hue with a chroma of at least 0.05.
- **A caption hid two failures.** The "Accent 20 %" text selection showed
  only the comment's ratio; keywords and numbers failed on it too, and
  the owner was asked before they were lightened (D-83).
- **Once a family of the gallery was cut by mistake** in place of the
  candidates; the tests caught it and the file was restored from git.
- **The owner's wallpaper switcher** (their commit bb8de46) was rewritten
  without a script, as the prototype's rules ask.

Left for later (my call):

  tokens file; P5.3 gives them a home when the decoration is generated.
- Whether Aurorae draws the eased fade and its insets is unverified
  (platform document); P5.3 tests it.
- The wallpapers load from invent.kde.org and need the network.
