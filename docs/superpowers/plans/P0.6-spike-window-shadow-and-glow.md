# P0.6 Spike: window shadow and glow Implementation Plan

**Goal:** Find out whether an Aurorae theme can draw a shadow of Amber's own around a window, and whether it can draw a subtle glow around a window, and make pictures of both for the owner.

**Asked for by:** the owner, on 2026-11-11, after spike P0.4 and the decision that a window has a window outline (D-25).

**Spec:** `docs/architecture/platform.md` ("Window decoration", its "Unverified" table and finding P0.4), `docs/architecture/design-language.md` ("Window decoration", "Glow and shadow"), `docs/architecture/decisions.md` (D-4, D-25, O-4), `docs/product/glossary.md` ("Glow", "Shadow").

**Approach:** The set-up of P0.4 again: a throwaway Aurorae theme `amber-spike` in `~/.local/share/aurorae/themes/`, selected only in a nested KWin with its own configuration, cache and D-Bus session. The shadow and the glow are drawn in the frame's SVG, in the room that the theme's padding keeps around the window. Test windows stand on a mid-tone backdrop, so that a dark shadow and a light glow can both be seen and measured.

## Global Constraints

- As in P0.4: no system setting is changed, `~/.config/kwinrc` and `~/.config/kdeglobals` keep their checksums, nothing is written under `/usr`, all spike files live in `tmp/spike-decoration/` and are not committed, the theme is removed at the end.
- The glossary says Amber has no glow, and D-4 says nothing glows. This spike only finds out what can be drawn. Whether a window glows is the owner's decision; this plan does not make it.
- A glow shows no state of a widget. If the owner wants one, what it means (the active window, or every window) is theirs to say; the spike pictures both.
- Starting amounts (my call): shadow black, 24 px, 50 % at the window's edge; glow accent `rgb(209, 176, 114)`, 14 px, 28 % at the window's edge.
- The anti-goals hold for the result: the window's corners stay square, and no line appears except the window outline.

## Tasks

- [x] **1. Rebuild the nested KWin set-up of P0.4** with a mid-tone backdrop, and picture Breeze's decoration on it as the baseline: how wide and how dark is its shadow?
- [x] **2. Draw a shadow.** Padding on all four sides, edge elements with a linear gradient from dark to nothing, corner elements with a radial one. Picture a floating window. Does a soft shadow show outside the window, and are the window's corners still square?
- [x] **3. Change the shadow:** another width, another strength, more room below than above. Does each change show? Try a blur filter in the SVG in place of the gradients.
- [x] **4. Draw a glow:** the same in accent, for the active window, with a plain dark shadow for the inactive one. Picture both windows, overlapping, so that the glow falls on the window behind.
- [x] **5. Put the window outline with it** (1 px, border size `Tiny`), and check the other window states: maximised (is the shadow or glow gone, and does the window fill the output?) and restored. Scale 1 and 1.49.
- [x] **6. Write the finding** into `docs/architecture/platform.md` ("Window decoration", the "Unverified" rows this answers, "Spike findings"). Set the roadmap row.
- [x] **7. Show the owner** a live nested window with the candidates, and the pictures. Then remove the theme and the spike's files except the pictures, and commit. Stop there: glow or no glow is the owner's decision.

**Done when:** as in the roadmap's row P0.6.

## Outcome — 2026-11-11

Both can be drawn. The owner saw the live window and decided that the
active window has a window glow and an inactive window a shadow (D-26).
